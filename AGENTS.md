# Project preferences

- Develop and verify UI changes on phone-sized screens first (360–430 px), then desktop.
- Keep controls compact; put secondary filters in a dismissible panel.
- Preserve loaded content when navigating or returning from the background. Photo caches must remain private and access-checked.
- Constrain form controls and textareas so they cannot expand their containers horizontally.
- Keep implementations focused and avoid unnecessary redesigns or lengthy tool output.
